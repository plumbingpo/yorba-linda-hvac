The Two Repairs We Get Called For Most

Most of the calls we answer come down to two things: a house that won't cool, or a house that won't heat. Here's how we handle both.

AC Repair in Yorba Linda

When your AC hums but won't cool, blows warm air, or keeps tripping the breaker, the fix is usually a single worn component. We test the system, find it, and replace it, often the same day. Pricing is upfront, and we'll tell you honestly whether a repair is worth it on an older unit.

  • AC running but blowing warm air
  • Outdoor unit humming but not starting
  • Ice on the refrigerant lines or indoor coil
  • Condensate backing up around the air handler

Furnace Repair in Yorba Linda

A furnace that won't light on a cold night can't wait until morning. Most no-heat calls come down to a dirty flame sensor, a failed igniter, or a blower motor, and our trucks carry the common parts to fix them on the spot. You'll get a clear quote first, plus a safety check of the combustion and venting while we're there.

  • No ignition when the heat calls
  • Burners light, then shut off within seconds
  • Blower running but the air isn't warm
  • Loud banging, squealing, or grinding at startup

A good rule of thumb: if the system is under 10 years old and the fix is a component, capacitor, motor, igniter, sensor, repair usually makes sense. If the compressor or heat exchanger has failed, the unit is past 12 to 15 years old, or repairs are stacking up season after season, replacement is the smarter investment. Our technicians walk you through both numbers honestly so you can decide with real information.
